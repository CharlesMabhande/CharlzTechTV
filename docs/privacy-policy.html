Privacy Policy

CharlzTechTV · Effective date: August 28, 2026

Developer: CharlzTech Software Developers (Charles Mabhande)

This Privacy Policy describes how CharlzTechTV (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android and Windows applications (com.charlztech.charlztechtv).

Summary: CharlzTechTV does not require an account, does not sell personal data, and stores most information locally on your device. The App needs internet access to load live sports listings and stream content from third-party sources.

1. Information We Collect

CharlzTechTV is designed to minimize data collection.

  • Information you save in the App — Favorites (channels/events you bookmark) are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Room database on Android; local file on Windows).
  • Cached content — The App may temporarily cache images, API responses, and playlist data on your device to improve performance.
  • Network activity — When you use the App, it connects to remote servers to fetch sports listings, channel metadata, and streaming URLs. Standard connection data (such as IP address) may be processed by those servers and your network provider.
  • Firebase Remote Config requests — The App may contact Google Firebase Remote Config to discover API endpoints. These requests can include anonymous technical identifiers (such as a randomly generated app instance ID), app/package metadata, and general device/platform information required by the Firebase API. We do not use Firebase Analytics in the App.

We do not collect:

  • Names, postal addresses, or government IDs
  • Payment or billing information
  • Precise location/GPS data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising profiles for targeted ads

2. How We Use Information

  • Display live sports events, channels, and streams
  • Save and restore your favorites on your device
  • Refresh content in the background and improve loading speed
  • Maintain playback features (including picture-in-picture and media playback services on Android)
  • Resolve API endpoints through Firebase Remote Config when available

3. Permissions (Android)

The Android app may request or use the following permissions:

  • Internet — Required to load content and streams.
  • Network state — Helps detect connectivity issues.
  • Foreground service / media playback — Supports continuous playback where applicable.
  • Wake lock — Helps prevent interruption during playback.

The App does not request location, contacts, storage (for personal files), or microphone permissions.

4. Third-Party Services and Content

CharlzTechTV retrieves listings and streams from third-party providers and content delivery networks. When you play content, those third parties may receive technical information such as your IP address, user agent, and requested URLs. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Third-party services that may be involved include:

  • Google Firebase Remote Config
  • Streaming/content API providers used by the App
  • Content hosts/CDNs referenced in playlists or event metadata

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only as needed to operate the App (for example, network requests to content/API providers and Firebase Remote Config), or when required by law.

6. Data Retention

  • Favorites and local cache remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the App.
  • Remote server logs (if any) are controlled by the respective third-party providers and are not managed by us.

7. Children’s Privacy

CharlzTechTV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.

9. Your Choices

  • Remove favorites at any time within the App.
  • Clear app cache from your device settings.
  • Uninstall the App to delete locally stored App data.
  • Disable background data or network access for the App in device settings.

10. International Users

The App may process and route data through servers located in different countries depending on third-party providers and your network path.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised when changes are posted. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
